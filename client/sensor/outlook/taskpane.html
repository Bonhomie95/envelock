This pane isn't running inside Outlook, so there's nothing for it to watch yet.

The sensor lives inside a message, not in the Apps list. Opening it from Apps, My Apps or the admin centre will always fail — that's Microsoft, not a fault in the add-in.

In Outlook:

  1. Open any email (the reading pane counts).
  2. On the ribbon above the message, click Envelock sensor. On Outlook for Mac and the web it may sit under the … (More apps) menu.
  3. The pane opens there and asks for your pairing code.
  4. Pin it with the pin at the top of the pane, so it stays open as you move between messages.

Just deployed it from the Microsoft 365 admin centre? Outlook can take up to 24 hours to show it. Removing it there is slower still — 24 to 72 hours — so an add-in you have already removed will keep appearing meanwhile. Restarting Outlook, or reloading Outlook on the web, often brings it forward.

You do not need to remove and re-add the add-in to pick up a new version of this pane: it is loaded fresh from Envelock each time it opens. Only a change to the ribbon button itself needs a redeploy.

Keep this pane pinned (the pin at the top) so it stays open as you move between messages. Envelock then knows every read here was you.
